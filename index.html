(06) How I work

Four moves, in order.

First we researchdirectbuildoptimise

01

Research the Product

Who it is for, what it replaces, and the detail a shopper actually uses to decide.

02

Develop the Creative Direction

A point of view: type, crop, colour, and the line the campaign is willing to repeat.

03

Build the E-commerce Experience

Store structure, listings, and product pages composed so the product stays understandable.

04

Optimise for Sales

Hierarchy, friction, and the path to purchase, revised once the thing is actually live.

Optimisation here means the structure and the presentation. What it earns depends on the product, the offer, and the traffic.
